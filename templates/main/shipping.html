Shipping & Delivery

At Zuhraan, we aim to process and deliver every order safely and efficiently. This Shipping & Delivery Policy explains how your order is processed, shipped, and delivered.

1. Order Processing

Once your order is successfully placed, we begin processing it.

Orders are generally processed within 1–3 business days, excluding Sundays and public holidays.

During high-demand periods, sales, or unforeseen circumstances, processing may take longer. In such cases, we will make reasonable efforts to keep you informed.

2. Shipping Time

Estimated Delivery Time

  • India: 3–7 business days

Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary depending on your location, courier availability, weather conditions, public holidays, or other circumstances beyond our control. Remote and difficult-to-reach locations may require additional delivery time.

3. Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, tracking details may be shared with you.

You can use the provided tracking information to check the status of your shipment.
